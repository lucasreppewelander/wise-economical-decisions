# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First release.

- When loot won't fit in your bags, a card next to the loot window suggests which bag item to drop and shows the gold you gain or lose.
- Three card states: **Suggested swap**, **Not worth it** (the closest swap) and **Make room for this** (quest items, keys and profession tools).
- Values use the vendor price, or the Auctionator price minus the 5% AH cut when it's at least 5× the vendor price. Bound items always use the vendor price.
- Profession tools, quest items, Hearthstone and teleport items, keys, ammo, soul shards and items with no price are never suggested as drops.
- Protect your own items with the **Never suggest this** button or `/wed protect`.
